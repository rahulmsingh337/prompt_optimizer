How to Get Better Results from Claude AI (2026)

📅 May 2026 ⏱ 6 min read Claude AI Anthropic

Claude, built by Anthropic, is one of the most capable AI assistants available. It excels at nuanced writing, long-document analysis, coding, and reasoning tasks. But like any AI, the quality of what you get out depends entirely on what you put in.

💡 Claude is particularly good at following detailed instructions. The more structured and specific your prompt, the more reliably Claude delivers exactly what you asked for.

How Claude Differs from ChatGPT

Understanding the differences helps you write better prompts for each platform:

  • Longer context: Claude can handle very long documents — use this by pasting full articles, codebases, or reports directly into your prompt
  • More literal: Claude tends to follow instructions very precisely — be explicit about every requirement
  • Better at nuance: Claude excels at tasks requiring careful reasoning — ask it to think through edge cases
  • Less likely to hallucinate on facts: Claude will often say it doesn't know rather than fabricate — useful for research tasks

Technique 1 — Use XML Tags for Structure

Claude responds especially well to structured prompts using XML-style tags. This is one of the most powerful Claude-specific techniques:

<task>Summarise this document</task>
<document>[paste your document here]</document>
<format>Bullet points, maximum 10 items</format>
<audience>Non-technical executives</audience>

The tags help Claude clearly identify each component of your request and process them independently before combining them into a response.

Technique 2 — Ask Claude to Think Aloud

For complex problems, start your prompt with "Think through this step by step before giving your final answer." Claude's chain-of-thought reasoning is particularly strong and catches errors that a direct answer would miss.

Technique 3 — Use Full Documents as Context

Claude's extended context window is its biggest advantage. Don't summarise documents before sending them — paste the full text. Claude can handle entire books, codebases, or research papers. Ask it to "read this carefully before answering" to ensure full processing.

Technique 4 — Be Explicit About What You Don't Want

Claude responds well to negative constraints. "Do not include caveats", "avoid passive voice", "don't start with a definition" — these explicit exclusions significantly clean up Claude's output, which can otherwise be verbose and hedge-heavy.

Technique 5 — Request Multiple Versions

Ask Claude to give you 2–3 variations of the same output with different tones or approaches. Claude handles this well and it's much faster than prompting separately for each version: "Give me three versions — one formal, one casual, one persuasive."

Best Use Cases for Claude

  • Summarising and analysing long documents
  • Writing and editing long-form content
  • Code review and refactoring
  • Legal and contract analysis
  • Research synthesis across multiple sources
  • Complex multi-step reasoning tasks
