25 AI Prompt Templates for Marketing (Copy-Ready 2026)

📅 May 2026⏱ 9 min readMarketingTemplates

The difference between a mediocre AI marketing output and one that actually converts is almost always the prompt. These 25 templates are structured, specific, and ready to paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini — or run through Prompify for further optimization. Replace the bracketed placeholders with your specific details.

💡 Tip: These templates work best when you replace every placeholder. The more specific your details, the stronger the output. Vague inputs produce vague results even with structured templates.

Email Marketing

1. Cold Outreach Email

"You are a senior B2B copywriter. Write a cold outreach email to [job title] at [company type] companies. Goal: schedule a 20-minute discovery call. Max 150 words. Include: one personalized opener referencing [specific trigger], the core value we offer in one sentence, and a low-friction CTA. Product: [describe]. Why relevant to them: [reason]. Tone: professional but direct."

2. Welcome Email Sequence (Email 1 of 3)

"Write the first email in a 3-part welcome sequence for new subscribers to [brand]. This email should: confirm their signup, deliver the promised [lead magnet/discount], introduce the brand in 2-3 sentences, and preview what they'll receive in the next emails. Tone: [warm/professional]. Max 200 words. Subject line included."

3. Re-engagement Email

"Write a re-engagement email for subscribers who haven't opened in 90 days. Subject line should create curiosity without being clickbait. Body: acknowledge the silence without guilt, share one piece of genuinely useful content or offer, give them an easy way to update preferences or unsubscribe. Brand: [name]. Tone: honest and human. Max 150 words."

4. Product Launch Announcement

"Write a product launch email for [product name] targeting [audience]. Structure: subject line with urgency element, opening hook addressing their #1 pain point, what the product does in one sentence, 3 key benefits (not features), social proof element, pricing/availability, single CTA. Tone: [excited/professional]. Launch date: [date]."

Social Media

5. LinkedIn Thought Leadership Post

"Write a LinkedIn post that positions me as a thought leader in [industry]. Format: hook in first line (no 'I' to start), 3-5 short paragraphs with white space, specific insight or contrarian take on [topic], personal experience that supports the insight, actionable takeaway, question to drive comments. Max 1,300 characters. No hashtags in body — 3 relevant hashtags at end only."

6. Twitter/X Thread

"Write a Twitter thread about [topic] for [audience]. Format: tweet 1 = bold hook that promises value, tweets 2-8 = numbered insights with one concrete example each, final tweet = summary and CTA to follow. Each tweet max 280 characters. Topic: [specific angle]. Make tweet 1 strong enough to stand alone as a single tweet."

7. Instagram Caption (Product)

"Write an Instagram caption for a photo of [product/scene description]. Target audience: [describe]. Include: a hook in the first line that stops the scroll, 2-3 sentences of storytelling or context, a subtle CTA, and 5 relevant hashtags. Tone: [aspirational/playful/educational]. Brand voice: [describe]. Max 150 words before hashtags."

8. Instagram Caption (Educational)

"Write an educational Instagram carousel caption for a post about [topic]. Audience: [describe]. Format: opening line that creates curiosity, 'save this for later' prompt, numbered list of [5-7] tips in short punchy sentences, closing CTA to follow for more. Tone: [friendly expert]. Include 5 niche hashtags."

Advertising

9. Google Search Ad (3 Variations)

"Write 3 Google search ad variations for [product/service] targeting the keyword '[keyword]'. Each ad needs: headline 1 (max 30 chars, keyword included), headline 2 (max 30 chars, benefit focused), headline 3 (max 30 chars, CTA), description 1 (max 90 chars, expand on benefit), description 2 (max 90 chars, social proof or urgency). Variation 1: benefit-led. Variation 2: problem-solution. Variation 3: social proof."

10. Facebook/Instagram Ad Copy

"Write Facebook ad copy for [product] targeting [audience demographics and interests]. Format: primary text (hook + problem + solution + CTA, max 125 chars before truncation), headline (max 40 chars), description (max 30 chars). Objective: [conversions/traffic/awareness]. Tone: [direct/emotional/humorous]. USP: [main differentiator]."

11. Retargeting Ad Copy

"Write retargeting ad copy for people who visited [page] but didn't convert. They already know who we are. Focus on: overcoming the most common objection ([objection]), reinforcing the key benefit they missed, adding urgency or social proof they haven't seen. Product: [name]. Offer: [if any]. Keep primary text under 100 words."

Content Marketing

12. SEO Blog Post Outline

"Create a detailed SEO blog post outline for the keyword '[target keyword]' with search intent: [informational/transactional/navigational]. Include: H1 title with keyword, meta description (max 155 chars), introduction hook, 5-7 H2 sections with 2-3 H3 subsections each, FAQ section with 5 questions, conclusion with CTA. Target word count: [1200/2000/3000]. Audience: [describe]."

13. Product Page Copy

"Write product page copy for [product name]. Sections needed: headline (benefit-focused, not product name), subheadline (expand on the promise), above-the-fold paragraph (who it's for and the transformation), 3 feature-benefit pairs, social proof section placeholder, FAQ with 4 questions and answers, CTA button text. Audience: [describe]. Tone: [confident/friendly/technical]."

14. Case Study Structure

"Write a case study for [client/company name] using our [product/service]. Structure: compelling headline with metric, client background (2 sentences), the challenge they faced before us, why they chose us over alternatives, implementation process (3-5 steps), quantified results with specific metrics ([X]% improvement in [metric]), direct client quote placeholder, conclusion with similar prospect CTA. Tone: credible and specific."

15. Video Script (60 seconds)

"Write a 60-second video script for [platform: YouTube/TikTok/Instagram] about [topic]. Format: 0-5s hook (pattern interrupt or bold statement), 5-45s main content (problem then solution in [X] steps), 45-55s proof or example, 55-60s CTA. Speaking pace: 150 words per minute. Audience: [describe]. Tone: [energetic/calm/authoritative]. Include [B-roll/visual] notes in brackets."

Brand and Strategy

16. Brand Voice Guidelines

"Create brand voice guidelines for [company name], a [type of company] serving [audience]. Include: 4 brand personality traits with 2-3 sentences explaining each, do/don't examples for each trait, vocabulary we use vs avoid, example sentences showing the brand voice in action for: social media, email, error messages, and customer support. Our values: [list]."

17. Competitor Analysis Summary

"Analyse the competitive landscape for [product category]. For each of these competitors: [list 3-5], cover: their positioning statement, target audience, key differentiators, pricing strategy (if known), main weakness. Then identify: gaps in the market none of them address, the positioning opportunity for [our brand], and 3 messages we could use to differentiate. Format as a structured report."

18. Press Release

"Write a press release announcing [news: product launch/partnership/funding/milestone] for [company name]. Format: headline (newsy, not promotional), dateline, lead paragraph (who, what, when, where, why in 2-3 sentences), body with supporting details and context (3 paragraphs), two executive quotes (placeholders with suggested angles), boilerplate about the company, contact information placeholder. Date: [date]."

Customer Communication

19. Customer Onboarding Message

"Write an onboarding message for new customers of [product/service]. Goal: get them to complete [key activation action] within 48 hours. Include: warm welcome, one sentence on what they've unlocked, the single most important first step with clear instructions, what success looks like, and how to get help. Tone: encouraging but concise. Max 200 words. Channel: [email/in-app/SMS]."

20. Negative Review Response

"Write a professional response to this negative review: '[paste review]'. The response should: acknowledge the frustration without being defensive, take responsibility for what went wrong, offer a specific resolution (not vague apologies), invite them to contact us directly to resolve it, and close positively. Tone: empathetic and professional. Max 100 words. Never offer compensation publicly."

Quick-Use Templates

21. Tagline (5 variations)

"Generate 5 tagline options for [brand name], a [description]. Each tagline: max 7 words, benefit-focused or emotionally resonant, avoids generic terms like 'innovative' or 'best-in-class'. Label each with the angle it uses: benefit, aspiration, problem, contrast, or curiosity."

22. Newsletter Subject Lines (10 options)

"Write 10 email subject lines for a newsletter about [topic/content]. Mix of approaches: curiosity gap (3), number-led (2), question (2), benefit-led (2), contrarian (1). Each under 50 characters. Preview text suggestion included for each. Audience: [describe]. Avoid: clickbait, excessive punctuation, ALL CAPS."

23. Testimonial Request Message

"Write a short message asking [customer type] for a testimonial. Make it easy by: telling them exactly what to write about (their situation before, the result they got, who they'd recommend us to), giving a word count (50-100 words), offering to draft something they can edit. Tone: grateful and specific, not generic. Channel: [email/SMS]. Include subject line."

24. Referral Program Email

"Write an email inviting existing customers to refer friends to [product]. Include: the specific referral offer ([X] for them, [Y] for the friend), one sentence on why they'd want to share this, the exact steps to refer (max 3), expiry date if applicable, CTA button text. Tone: friendly and excited without being pushy. Max 150 words."

25. Event Invitation

"Write an invitation email for [event name], a [type: webinar/conference/workshop] on [topic]. Include: subject line with date, opening that creates FOMO, what attendees will learn or gain (3 bullet points), speaker credential (one sentence), logistical details (date, time, location/link), early bird or limited availability element if applicable, RSVP CTA. Audience: [describe]. Tone: [professional/exciting]."
